# Invite messages (WhatsApp)

Replace the brackets. Keep it personal: send from a team member's own number.

## First invite

> Good [morning/afternoon] [Pastor/Rev./Evangelist] [Name]. I'm [your name] from FutureLabs. We're building OpenPulpit, a simple way for preachers to go live with audio from their phone, so people can listen live and replay sermons, using very little data (about 15 MB an hour).
>
> We're inviting a few preachers to try it before anyone else, free. It takes 5 minutes to set up and we'll do it with you on a short call. Would you be open to trying it for the next four weeks?

## After they say yes

> Thank you, [Name]! Here's your link: [beta link]. When is a good time this week for a 20-minute call? We'll set up your profile together and do a short test broadcast. You'll just need your phone and a quiet room.

## For their listeners (they forward this)

> I'm now preaching live on OpenPulpit. Follow me and you'll be told when I go live, and you can replay past sermons. It uses very little data. [profile link]
