# OpenPulpit: getting started (for preachers)

Share this with beta preachers on WhatsApp, or read it with them on the onboarding call.

## 1. Sign up (2 minutes)

1. Open **[your beta link]** in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
2. Tap **Sign in → Create an account**. Enter your name and email, choose **"I'm a preacher"**, and accept the guidelines.
3. Add your profile: the name people know you by, your ministry or church, your state and city. Add a photo; it helps people recognise you.

Tip: on Android, tap **Install** on the home page. On iPhone, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**. OpenPulpit then opens like an app.

## 2. Go live (1 minute)

1. Tap **Go live** at the bottom.
2. Type a title (for example "Sunday Service"). The Bible passage is optional.
3. Tap **Check my microphone** and say a few words. When it says **"We can hear you"**, you're ready.
4. Tap **Go live**, then **Go live** again to confirm. Your followers get a notification.

While you're live you can see how many people are listening and how long you've been going. Tap **End broadcast** when you're done. Your sermon is saved on your profile within a minute or two.

## Tips for a good broadcast

- **Phone close to your mouth**, about a hand's width away. Keep it on a stand or table, not in your pocket.
- **Plug in the charger.** The screen stays on while you're live.
- **Wi-Fi or a strong 4G signal is best.** If the network drops for a moment, don't stop: keep talking. OpenPulpit reconnects on its own and you stay live for up to a minute.
- **Don't play recorded worship songs** you don't have rights to. Live singing by your choir is fine.
- **Free plan:** 10 hours of broadcasting a month, and your 20 newest sermons are kept. The app shows how many hours you have left.

## Grow your audience

- Share your profile link (on your profile, tap **WhatsApp**). People who follow you get told when you go live.
- After each sermon, share its link in your church's WhatsApp groups; it shows a preview card.
