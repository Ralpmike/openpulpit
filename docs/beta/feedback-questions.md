# Feedback questions

## Preachers (calls in week 2 and week 4, about 15 minutes)

1. How easy was it to go live the first time? What slowed you down?
2. Did anything go wrong during a broadcast (sound, connection, the app closing)? When?
3. How did your listeners find you? Did they follow you and get notifications?
4. How many people listened, compared with what you expected?
5. Did you use your recordings: rename, hide, share links? Was anything missing?
6. Did the monthly hour limit or the 20-recording limit get in your way?
7. What would make you preach on OpenPulpit every week?
8. Would you recommend it to another preacher? Why or why not?

## Listeners (short WhatsApp survey, forwarded by the preacher)

1. What phone do you use, and do you usually listen on mobile data or Wi-Fi?
2. Did the sound play clearly? Did it stop or break up?
3. Could you keep listening with your screen off or while using another app?
4. Did you get a notification when the preacher went live?
5. Roughly how much data did an hour of listening use? (Settings → Network → Data usage)
6. Did you replay a sermon? Was it easy to find your place again?
7. Anything confusing or annoying?
